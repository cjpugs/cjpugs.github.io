Concept
Overgrown is a dialogueless co-op puzzle game about the impacts of climate change in a world left behind by humans. An unlikely duo forms between a raccoon and a robot, and they must solve puzzles to navigate the world and reach safety.
Contributions
  • Created 150+ sound effects through Foley recording.
  • Composed two songs for the game soundtrack.
  • Edited all sound effect and music assets through iterative design.
Key Takeaways
  • Producing assets for a team using sprint structure.
  • Prioritizing assets based on the game's needs.
  • Recording and editing Foley sound effects effectively.
