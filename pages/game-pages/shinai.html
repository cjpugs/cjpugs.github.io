Concept
Shin'ai Rewritten is a cozy life-sim game where you reconnect with your grandfather in the Japanese countryside, while breathing new life into the rural community.
All of the music was written over the course of six months. Each in-game day is split into Morning, Noon, Evening, and Night, and most of the music acts as ambience to one of these four time periods.
Contributions
  • Created 15 songs and 10 musical stingers.
  • Revised and mixed all music using feedback from the team.
Key Takeaways
  • Write, compose, and edit music quickly.
  • Create a full soundtrack with a unifying tone.
