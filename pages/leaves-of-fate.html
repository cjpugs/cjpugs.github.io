Concept
Leaves of Fate is a narrative rich platforming game where you play as Fenn, a young kid who has always dreamed of becoming a Knight. During a visit to the Palace, you discover that nefarious wizards have sealed the Knights away, and it’s up to you to save them!
This game was created in Godot Engine over the course of four months by a team of twelve developers.
Contributions
  • Drafted and iterated on the narrative structure and all game dialogue.
  • Utilized the Dialogue Manager plugin for Godot to script cutscene sequences.
  • Composed and iterated on all game music.
  • Managed a 12-person team using a weekly sprint structure.
Key Takeaways
  • Playtesting and iterating on dialogue to meet playtesting deadlines.
  • Writing concisely to keep the player informed and engaged.
  • Creating suitable game assets instead of aiming for perfection.
  • Adjusting production tools to support team organization.
